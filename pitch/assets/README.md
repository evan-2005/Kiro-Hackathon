# Kuala Lumpur vision demo pair

`kl-before.jpg` is an Esri World Imagery export. `kl-after-synthetic.png` is an AI-generated flood composite of that export, not an observation of a real flood. Do not describe it as Sentinel imagery or confirmed disaster evidence.

Source: [Esri World Imagery](https://services.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer). Attribution: Esri, Vantor, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community. The export's returned extent and URL are retained in `imagery-export.json`. Review the source's terms before redistribution.

Both images cover the full mission extent, north-up, in EPSG:4326: west 101.66, south 3.11, east 101.73, north 3.17. The original is 1120 × 960 pixels and the generated composite is 1355 × 1161. Compare normalized positions, not raw pixels. The composite preserves the broad frame visually but is not survey-grade registered imagery.

The synthetic muddy-water patch follows the central river corridor around the Klang-Gombak confluence and downstream bridge crossings. Generated imagery cannot establish real closures, water depth, or safe access.
